About me

Business development, built on research, outreach and technology fluency.

I'm Sabrina Muqadas, a Growth & Business Development Executive who builds pipeline across four channels: Upwork bidding, LinkedIn social selling, email outreach and cold calling.

My focus is multi-channel B2B and B2C lead generation, client acquisition and AI-driven workflow optimization for technology companies in the USA, UK, UAE and KSA.

My path began in talent acquisition and content at DEFT Recruiter, sourcing and screening candidates for oil and gas roles across the Gulf. That grounding in communication, qualification and the Gulf market still shapes how I approach every prospect.

At Sigma Digital Solutions I led lead generation and data management as KYCIER Team Lead: qualifying leads with Sales Navigator, Apollo and RocketReach, running UI/UX and SEO audits, and mentoring teammates on pipeline tracking. Strong results in data optimization and client engagement led to my promotion to Upwork Bidder, where I wrote tailored proposals, optimized profiles and catalogs, and used AI tools to move faster.

In 2026 I broadened into outbound cold calling at AEITCH and joined ISOTOPE as Growth & Business Development Executive, pitching AI products, Python development, DevOps infrastructure and full-stack applications to international clients.

I translate technical requirements into clear solutions, manage pipelines in HubSpot and Salesforce, and use AI tools such as ChatGPT and Grok to maximize campaign ROI, while keeping the data clean and the conversations human.
